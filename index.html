About Filter X

Know what's inside a book before you read it.

What is Filter X?

Filter X is a book guide for readers who like to choose carefully. For each book you can see its spice level, how much violence it has, and content warnings such as cheating, domestic violence or gore, along with its tropes and mood. Then you can filter, so you only see books that suit you.

How to use it

  • Browse to filter by spice, violence, tropes and things you want to avoid.
  • The flames show spice: more flames means more heat.
  • Try Surprise me or Blind Date to discover a book without seeing its title first.
  • Save books in My Library as favorites, bookmarks, or what you're reading.
  • Open any book to read its details, write a review, or add a private note.

How accurate is it?

Ratings and warnings are put together by hand and may be incomplete or occasionally wrong. Everyone's comfort level is different, so please treat them as a guide, not a guarantee. If you spot a mistake, tell us in a review or through "Request a book".

Your privacy

Your favorites, bookmarks, reading status and private notes stay on your own device. We can't see them. We count visits and popular searches with a privacy-friendly tool that uses no cookies and collects no personal details. If you send us a book request or a review, we receive what you type in it.
